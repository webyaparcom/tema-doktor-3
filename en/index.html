What people ask before a visit

8questions

How is an appointment confirmed?Appointmentsexpand_more

Your chosen day and time are sent as a request; the front desk calls to confirm, usually within half an hour in opening hours.

How do I change my appointment?Appointmentsexpand_more

Call the front desk or write on WhatsApp; letting us know at least a day before makes it easier to find a new time.

What should I bring?Visitsexpand_more

Bring your ID, a list of the medicines you take and any previous test results.

How long does a visit take?Visitsexpand_more

The slot is 20 minutes; if the physician sees a need, an extra appointment is planned.

Should I fast before a blood test?Testsexpand_more

Some tests need 8–12 hours of fasting; your physician or the laboratory will tell you which ones.

How do I get my results?Testsexpand_more

Routine results are ready the next working day and reviewed with the physician at the follow-up visit.

Is private health insurance accepted?Insuranceexpand_more

Ask the front desk for the list of contracted insurers; coverage depends on your policy (fictional information).
