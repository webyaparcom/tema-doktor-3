Departments

Department index

Six departments, one registration

Hover over or tap a row; the department’s scope opens in the file beside it. This is general information; every visit is assessed by a physician.

  1. stethoscope

    01

    Internal medicine

    Adult examinations; follow-up of blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid and cholesterol; referral to a specialist centre when needed.

  2. monitor_heart

    02

    Cardiology

    ECG, exercise test and echocardiography are done on a physician’s request; results are explained at the visit.

  3. child_care

    03

    Paediatrics

    Baby and child check-ups, growth monitoring and keeping the vaccination schedule.

  4. pregnant_woman

    04

    Women’s health

    Routine gynaecological examinations, pregnancy follow-up and screening appointments.

  5. hearing

    05

    Ear, nose and throat

    Ear, nose and throat examinations; hearing screening is planned after a physician’s assessment.

  6. biotech

    06

    Laboratory

    Blood samples on a physician’s request; routine results are ready the next working day and reviewed together at the visit.
